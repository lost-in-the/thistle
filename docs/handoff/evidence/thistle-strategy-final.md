## Why the approach is coherent

The product preserves useful customer jobs and their evidence quality while permitting independent commands, APIs, schemas, screens, and package boundaries. That correctly reflects the latest direction: launch outcome parity without old-tool support. Woods is a comparator and development instrument, not a required runtime or the sole truth oracle.

Map reuse is explicitly authorized and useful. The candidate permits reusing its implementation, assets, design, and branding while replacing Woods reader, policy, launcher, and other runtime dependencies. The candidate-only installed-bundle gate prevents a superficially new product from depending on Woods underneath. The plan also correctly removes dual-provider and legacy-API maintenance as obligations.

The three strongest bets are now clear: shared trustworthy evidence with appropriate projections, a focused investigation/change workbench, and elimination of measured repeated work. Their value is expressed as correct completed tasks with less effort or cost. The plan does not mistake a new storage format, language, diagram, or number of tools for a proven differentiator.

The first-principles challenge table contains counterarguments and proof obligations, including the possibility that an existing technique is already the right one. It specifically avoids inferring repeated parsing from extractor count. It retains a simpler reference path and makes broader optimization conditional on a measured bottleneck. This is a credible critical competitor approach rather than a disguised implementation clone or an unlimited feature list.

## Launch-bar assessment

The candidate includes both the published minimum and useful implemented current behavior, with status and scope pinned in a capability ledger. It prohibits an attractive narrow prototype from qualifying as the requested launch. Conditional workflows are assessed as customer jobs rather than dismissed because their wiring is optional. Deprecated integrations and unimplemented promises do not automatically become obligations.

The ledger and independent oracles protect against both kinds of false parity: copying a comparator defect and hiding an omission behind an aggregate score. Support environments, extraction breadth, human investigations, programmatic access, semantic and lexical retrieval, full-source follow-up, lifecycle, exports, and deployment jobs all remain visible.

The practical quality rule is appropriately bounded. Required deterministic behavior admits no tolerance. For stochastic tasks, a no-observed-regression floor and independently justified uncertainty limits apply to a finite set of customer-job strata and the aggregate. Failed, unset, or inconclusive required gates block launch. Claims identify their tested workloads and margins; strict superiority requires evidence beyond zero. This supports an operational launch decision without pretending to prove universal equality.

## Performance and self-improvement claims

Performance is evaluated from installation and boot through fresh public-client output and successful tasks. The accounting includes provider calls, failed attempts, source expansion, maintenance, retained generations, all owned processes, and multi-project resource costs. SQLite, residency, native kernels, native query execution, and custom storage must earn their added complexity.

Progressive evidence remains a tested hypothesis, with explicit omissions and complete-source expansion. Shared parsing is tied to actual repeated work and correct input invalidation. Neither a smaller initial response nor deferred computation is allowed to create fake speed through reduced coverage.

The introspective loop begins with customer-visible failures or bottlenecks, uses source/profile evidence, proposes a bounded patch, and submits an immutable artifact to independent evaluation. At least one complete cycle must operate before launch. The plan accurately distinguishes that demonstration from evidence that agents are more productive or that installed software improves itself autonomously. Independent ownership of evaluation policy, untouched confirmation data, and retained failed experiments address feedback-loop overfitting.

## Interaction and packaging assessment

The naming direction prioritizes recognizable words and visible actions rather than forcing every term onto the home row. Shortcuts are optional, configurable, focus-aware, and disabled in text/dictation contexts. Fuzzy discovery cannot silently change exact code identities or actions. Intended-user evaluation remains necessary; the plan makes no medical-effectiveness claim.

One obvious product installation is the default. An optional UI gem is justified by dependency and lifecycle needs, not by an arbitrary requirement to split the project. Working-name and registry availability are explicitly distinguished. Those decisions preserve flexibility without introducing another setup taxonomy for users to learn.
